      Practical skills

      The Knight Club courses are made for learning skills you can put to work — on a client project, a piece of content, a small business or a new career direction. The focus is on doing, not just watching. Each course has its own page with the instructor, level, curriculum and price, so you can judge a course before you commit.

      Who the courses are for

      Students building skills beside their studies, freelancers, creators, career switchers and anyone who wants to learn digital skills by doing. A focused skill course can fill gaps that formal education may not cover. You don’t need to be an expert to begin — beginner-friendly options are marked by level on each course.

      How learning works

      Courses are broken into modules and lessons you follow in order. You learn a step, apply it, then move on, so each idea builds on the last and you build one skill step by step instead of jumping between disconnected videos. See how we approach learning, or read our learning philosophy.

      What to check before enrolling

      Read the curriculum, check the instructor and price, and make sure the level fits you. Want to see what other learners think? Visit our student stories, or contact us with any question.
